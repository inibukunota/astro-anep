Jompa is a new Malaysian-made app that analyses everyone’s location and estimated travel time before suggesting a meetup point that is fair for everyone.

Picking a place to meet up with friends can sometimes get surprisingly complicated. One person might be too far away, while another may have limited transport options. Ideally, you want a meetup location that makes things as convenient as possible for everyone.

## What is Jompa?

Jompa is a meetup location finder that helps friends, families, and colleagues decide where to meet based on each person’s location and travel time.

All you need to do is share your location and preferred mode of transport with Jompa. For privacy, participants cannot see each other’s current locations. They can only see the estimated arrival time.

Jompa works across Malaysia and can be accessed through a web browser or mobile device without requiring an account. This makes it quick and easy to find a suitable place to hang out.

## How to use Jompa

According to its tutorial, getting started with Jompa is pretty straightforward.

1. One person creates a meetup session and shares the invitation link with everyone.
2. Each participant opens the link and enters their location and mode of transport.
3. Jompa calculates the midpoint, estimated arrival times, and suggests several meetup locations.
4. Everyone votes for their preferred location.
5. The host finalises the meetup location.

One interesting thing about Jompa is that it does not simply look for the geographical midpoint. Its algorithm also considers other factors, such as traffic and travel conditions.

That means the recommended location may not necessarily be exactly halfway between everyone.

## Why Jompa is interesting

Jompa’s algorithm puts more emphasis on the person who has the longest or most difficult journey, helping reduce the amount of travelling they need to do.

For example, two people might be travelling roughly the same distance to a particular location. However, one person could still take much longer to get there because of traffic or because they need to rely on public transport.

So, you no longer have to be the friend who ends up driving halfway across the country just to meet everyone.

Jompa could be useful for:

* Friends planning dinner, a teh tarik session, or a weekend hangout.
* Families who live far apart from each other.
* Vendors and clients looking for a convenient location for project meetings.

## Should you try Jompa?

There is little reason not to give Jompa a try since you do not have to go through an account registration process.

Just open it in Safari or Chrome and get started.

If your group is constantly stuck arguing over where to meet, Jompa might be worth a shot. With everyone’s location and travel time taken into account, there is hopefully less arguing over who has to travel the furthest or deal with the worst traffic.
